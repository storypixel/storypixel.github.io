Describe a play

Plain English in, DBN notation out, animated play anywhere.

1. Describe your play

Say it like a coach would. Who has balls, who moves where, who throws at whom, what happens.

2. Copy the prompt

Copy this prompt, paste it into whatever AI you use — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — and paste the notation it returns into step 3. The prompt carries the live rules, so your AI needs no other context.

The rules it teaches your AI:

  • The notation rules — every token, formation, and outcome, with exact semantics
  • The rules for AI agents — tell an agent “Using this URL, make me a play where…” and it does all of this itself
  • Glossary — the dodgeball terms
  • Automation guide — deep links, window API, headless CLI, validate endpoint

3. The notation

Paste the notation your AI returned. It validates and animates as you type — edit freely.

4. Share it

Paste this snippet into any web page — the play animates in place. Or open it in the editor to keep tweaking.
