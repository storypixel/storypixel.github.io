# Callbook authoring

Feature: market Callbook and let a coach enter, preview, save and share plays
without first learning DBN. Requested by Sam after approving the language push.

Now: guided setup/actions/conditions, raw DBN mode, local library, imports,
downloads and explicit share links; update existing /callbook/ marketing page.
Later: accounts, cloud playbook publishing, full rules engine, AI generation.
Preserve the team playbook, existing notation editor and copy-prompt composer.

Design: coaches checking a bright phone courtside or writing plays at a desk.
Light paper, dark ink, red ball accent, blue links; readable serif marketing
and ordinary system-font editing controls. No ornamental cards or testimonials.
The court stays in its isolated shadow root. Actual play artwork is the visual.

Acceptance: validated DBN round trip, usable mobile/desktop, saves survive reload,
invalid source cannot share stale output, imports preserve raw source, useful
clipboard/storage errors, no unrequested network or model calls, regression tests.

Authority: user requested push and these new surfaces. Stage only this work.
