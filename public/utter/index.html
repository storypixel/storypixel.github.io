How it works

Stay in the thought. Leave the keyboard alone.

01

Turn it on

Click the menu-bar dot or press Control–Option–Space. The live ring shows when Utter hears you.

02

Talk normally

Pause when you finish a thought. Utter transcribes it locally and types into the app you already have open.

03

Say what happens next

End with “enter” to send, switch apps by name, select text, rewrite it, or clear the box without touching a key.

Voice commands

Your words are the shortcuts.

Utter understands the phrases you would naturally say. It also handles close speech-recognition guesses when you switch apps, so “Chat GBT” still gets you to ChatGPT.

“enter” “never mind” “switch to Safari” “select all” “make it friendlier” “stop listening”

Built for real work

Fast enough to disappear into your day.

◎

Only your voice

Optional on-device voice learning helps ignore calls, videos, and people nearby. Short clips fail open so quick commands still work.

⌘

Safe by default

Utter avoids password fields, will not press Return in terminals by default, and restores your clipboard after typing.

AI

On-device rewriting

On Macs with Apple Intelligence, select text by voice and describe the change. Utter rewrites that exact selection and never sends it to a cloud model.

↻

Mic-aware

Remember preferred microphones, fall back when one disconnects, and automatically skip a wireless receiver that is only sending silence.

•••

Make the language yours

Change the phrases that send, clear, rewrite, pause, or resume. Utter shows the meaning of every command before you save it.

Private by design

Your voice is not the product.

Audio, transcripts, selected text, and voice recognition stay on your Mac. Utter has no account system, advertising, analytics, or cloud transcription.
