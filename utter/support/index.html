First setup

  1. Open Utter and allow Microphone access.
  2. Enable Utter in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  3. Let the first speech model download finish. It is about 630 MB.
  4. Click into any text field, turn Utter on from the menu bar, and start talking.

Utter lives in the menu bar. You can also turn it on and off with Control–Option–Space.

Utter cannot hear me

  • Open Utter’s panel and confirm the switch is on and the status says Listening.
  • Choose the correct microphone from the bottom of the panel.
  • In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, make sure Utter is enabled.
  • If a wireless receiver is selected, confirm its transmitter is on. Utter will skip inputs that only send digital silence.
  • Calls temporarily take priority over the mic. Utter resumes automatically when the call ends.

Utter hears me but does not type

  • Enable Utter in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  • Click back into the text field where you want the words to appear.
  • Utter deliberately does not type into password or secure fields.
  • Utter will not press Return in terminal apps unless you remove that app from the ignored list.
  • If dictation is paused, say “start listening.” If it is fully off, click the menu-bar dot or use the keyboard shortcut.

Only My Voice

Choose Learn My Voice from Utter’s menu and read the five sentences. The resulting voice profile stays on your Mac. Choose Relearn My Voice to replace it, or turn Only My Voice off from the same menu.

Very short commands are allowed through even when voice filtering is on, because there is not enough audio for a reliable voice match.

Updates

Release builds use Sparkle to check for signed Utter updates. Choose Check for Updates from the menu at any time. You can also download the newest notarized version from the public release page.

Reset or remove local data

Utter stores its models, preferences, and optional voice profile only on this Mac.

Replace the voice profile Choose Relearn My Voice from Utter’s menu.
Remove the voice profile Quit Utter, then delete ~/Library/Application Support/Utter/voice-profile.json.
Remove downloaded models Quit Utter, then delete the models inside ~/Library/Application Support/Utter.
Uninstall Quit Utter and move Utter.app to the Trash. Remove the Application Support folder if you also want to delete local models and the voice profile.

System requirements

  • Apple silicon Mac
  • macOS 14 or later
  • About 630 MB for the initial speech model, plus a smaller voice-recognition model if you enable Only My Voice
  • Microphone and Accessibility permissions
  • Apple Intelligence on macOS 26 or later for optional on-device rewriting
